SUPPLY AGREEMENT — MEHTA TRADERS PVT. LTD.
Contract Reference: MT-2025-0042
Effective Date: 01-Jan-2025
Expiry Date: 31-Dec-2025
Parties: Mehta Traders Pvt. Ltd. ("Supplier") and Procurement AI Control Tower ("Buyer")

1. SCOPE OF SUPPLY
Mehta Traders agrees to supply the following categories:
  - Industrial Fasteners (M6, M8, M10 steel screws, bolts, and nuts)
  - Mild Steel Plates (A36 grade, 4x8 ft sheets, 6mm and 10mm thickness)
  - Welding Consumables (E6013 electrodes, MIG wire 0.8mm)

2. MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITY (MOQ)
  - Fasteners: 5,000 pieces per SKU per order
  - Steel Plates: 50 sheets per order
  - Welding Consumables: 100 kg per order
Orders below MOQ will incur a 12% small-order surcharge.

3. PRICING AND PAYMENT TERMS
  - Steel Screws M8: ₹4.50 per piece (FOB Mehta Warehouse, Mumbai)
  - Steel Plates 6mm: ₹8,200 per sheet
  - Steel Plates 10mm: ₹11,500 per sheet
  - Welding Electrodes E6013: ₹185 per kg
  - Payment Terms: Net 30 days from invoice date
  - Early Payment Discount: 2% discount if paid within 10 days
  - Late Payment Penalty: 1.5% per month on overdue balance

4. DELIVERY AND LOGISTICS
  - Standard Lead Time: 5 business days for fasteners, 7 business days for steel plates
  - Expedited Shipping: Available at 15% premium, reduces lead time to 2 business days
  - Free Shipping: Orders above ₹50,000 qualify for free standard delivery
  - Shipping Point: Mehta Traders Depot, Bhiwandi, Maharashtra

5. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
  - On-Time Delivery Rate Target: 95% per quarter
  - Quality Acceptance Rate Target: 98% per batch
  - Late Delivery Penalty: 2.5% of order value per day of delay beyond committed lead time
  - Maximum Penalty Cap: 15% of total order value
  - Defective Goods: Supplier must replace rejected batches within 3 business days at no cost
  - Force Majeure: Delays due to natural disasters, strikes, or government actions are exempt from penalties with documented proof within 48 hours

6. QUALITY STANDARDS
  - All fasteners must conform to IS 1364 (Part 1) standards
  - Steel plates must carry original mill test certificates
  - Welding consumables must meet AWS A5.1 specifications
  - Buyer reserves right to inspect goods at supplier premises with 24-hour notice

7. CONTRACT RENEWAL
  - Auto-renewal for 12 months unless either party provides 60-day written termination notice
  - Price revision capped at 5% annually, subject to mutual agreement
