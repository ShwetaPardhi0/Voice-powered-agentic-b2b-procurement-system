INVENTORY MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document ID: INV-POL-2025-001
Version: 2.3
Last Updated: 15-Mar-2025
Approved By: VP Supply Chain Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the rules and procedures for managing inventory levels across all warehouses. It ensures optimal stock availability while minimizing carrying costs and preventing stockouts that disrupt production schedules.

2. INVENTORY CLASSIFICATION (ABC ANALYSIS)
  - Category A (High Value): Top 20% of SKUs contributing 80% of annual spend
    → Reviewed weekly, reorder point calculated daily
  - Category B (Medium Value): Next 30% of SKUs contributing 15% of annual spend
    → Reviewed bi-weekly, reorder point calculated weekly
  - Category C (Low Value): Bottom 50% of SKUs contributing 5% of annual spend
    → Reviewed monthly, reorder point calculated monthly

3. REORDER RULES
  3.1 Reorder Point Formula:
      Reorder Point = (Average Daily Usage × Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock

  3.2 Safety Stock Formula:
      Safety Stock = Z-score × σ(demand) × √(Lead Time)
      Where Z-score = 1.65 for 95% service level (Category A items)
      Where Z-score = 1.28 for 90% service level (Category B items)
      Where Z-score = 1.04 for 85% service level (Category C items)

  3.3 Economic Order Quantity (EOQ):
      EOQ = √((2 × Annual Demand × Order Cost) / Holding Cost per Unit)

4. STOCK LEVEL THRESHOLDS
  - Maximum Stock Level: 3 months of average consumption
  - Minimum Stock Level: Equal to safety stock quantity
  - Critical Level (Emergency): When stock falls below 50% of safety stock
  - Dead Stock Threshold: Items with zero movement for 180+ days

5. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS FOR PURCHASE ORDERS
  - Up to ₹25,000: Auto-approved by system
  - ₹25,001 to ₹1,00,000: Procurement Manager approval required
  - ₹1,00,001 to ₹5,00,000: Senior Procurement Manager approval required
  - Above ₹5,00,000: VP Supply Chain approval required
  - Emergency Orders (any value): Procurement Manager can approve with post-facto VP notification within 24 hours

6. WAREHOUSE-SPECIFIC RULES
  - Mumbai Warehouse (WH-MUM): Primary hub, maintains 60% of total inventory
  - Delhi Warehouse (WH-DEL): Regional hub, maintains 25% of total inventory
  - Chennai Warehouse (WH-CHE): Regional hub, maintains 15% of total inventory
  - Inter-warehouse transfers require Manager approval and 48-hour processing time

7. CYCLE COUNTING AND AUDITS
  - Category A items: Full count every month
  - Category B items: Full count every quarter
  - Category C items: Full count every 6 months
  - Variance tolerance: 2% for Category A, 5% for Category B/C
  - Variances exceeding tolerance trigger immediate investigation

8. OBSOLESCENCE AND WRITE-OFF
  - Slow-moving stock (no demand for 90 days): Flag for review
  - Dead stock (no demand for 180 days): Initiate disposal or discount sale process
  - Write-off authority: Finance Controller for items under ₹50,000, CFO for above
